AISEO, Defined

WHAT IS
AI SEARCH OPTIMIZATION?

AI Search Optimization, or AISEO, is the practice of improving how a business is discovered, understood, represented, and cited in AI-powered search experiences.

It overlaps with traditional SEO but expands the focus beyond ranking webpages.

AISEO also looks at entity clarity, answer-ready content, structured information, third-party evidence, local signals, and how a brand appears when AI systems synthesize an answer.
